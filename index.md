# Public Health, Cancer Surveillance & Disaster Epidemiology
Welcome. I’m **Rauf Shah, MS, PhD** — **Lead Health Data Coordinator II** in oncology and an **MPH–Epidemiology** student.  
My work connects clinical oncology workflows with surveillance systems, FHIR data exchange, ctDNA pipelines, and population analytics.

> **Focus areas**

> • Lymphoma & ctDNA pipelines (copy number, panel design, registry integration)  
> • Disaster Epidemiology (situational awareness, syndromic feeds, dashboards)  
> • Translation to practice (from datasets → insights → policy & care improvement)
