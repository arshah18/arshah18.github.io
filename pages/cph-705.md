# CPH 705 — Assignments & Materials

## Needs Assessment — Cancer Surveillance Training (Module)
**Target Audience**: Graduate public health students, oncology fellows, and research professionals working with cancer surveillance data.  
**Key Characteristics**: Mixed backgrounds (clinical vs. data); need hands-on, case-based learning tied to real workflows and coding standards.

**Existing Data Sources**: Course evals, observations, surveys/logs, feedback from registry liaisons, related syllabi.  
**New Data**: Pre/post surveys, interviews, focus groups, observations, skill checks.  
**Collection Methods**: Online forms, guided interviews/focus groups (Zoom/Teams + transcription), lab skill checks, Miro for collaboration, Airtable for tracking.  
**Analysis Plan**: Descriptive stats; paired t-tests/chi-square; dashboards (Tableau/Data Studio); thematic analysis of transcripts; benchmark vs. prior cohorts.  
**Sharing & Recommendations**: Report + charts, slide deck, dashboards, emails, meetings, sample plans—continuous improvement.

## Backward Design — Intro to Public Health Surveillance Systems (Cancer/Lymphoma)
**Outcomes**  
- Explain surveillance goals; identify collection & coding processes.  
- Describe lymphoma registry capture; analyze incidence/survival trends.  
- Evaluate how findings inform research, policy, patient care.

**Evidence**  
- Data interpretation quiz; case study analysis; policy brief; article peer-review; reflection journal.

**Activities**  
- Lecture on registries/data sources; hands-on portal labs (CDC, SEER, OnCore); group analysis of reports; guest registry epidemiologist; policy-brief workshop.
