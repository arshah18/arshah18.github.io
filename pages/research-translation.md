# Research Interests & Translation to Practice

## Themes
- **Lymphoma & ctDNA**: copy-number–anchored panel design; integration of liquid biopsy with registry outcomes.  
- **Cancer Surveillance**: registry pipelines, coding standards (ICD, ICD-O, NAACCR), timeliness/quality evaluation.  
- **Population Analytics**: disparities, geospatial patterns, survival trends; dashboards for clinical and policy users.

## Approach
1. **Data linkage** — FHIR/HAPI workflows to align EHR, registry, and molecular data; versioned transformations.  
2. **Analytic pipelines** — reproducible R/Python for QC, modeling, and reporting (dashboards + validated tables).  
3. **Interpretation & review** — co-production with clinicians/registry partners; documentation of assumptions and limits.  
4. **Translation** — briefs and visual summaries aligned to program needs; measures tied to guidelines and equity.

## Impact Goals
- Reduce time from raw data to actionable insights.  
- Improve transparency and reproducibility for stakeholders.  
- Inform targeted interventions, trial design, and policy decisions.
