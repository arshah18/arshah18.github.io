# Research Interests & Translation to Practice
**Themes**: ctDNA/lymphoma genomics, registry integration, rapid analytics for clinicians, and evaluation of policy impacts.

**Reflections**  
- Bridge **bench findings** (molecular/ctDNA signals) to **patient-level decisions** via registries and dashboards.  
- Design studies that **co-produce** knowledge with clinicians and registry staff.  
- Emphasize **reproducibility**, versioning, and clear coding standards to keep findings credible and usable.
