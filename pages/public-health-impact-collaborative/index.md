# Public Health Impact Collaborative

{: .lead }
A founding public health initiative focused on practical projects, infectious disease and chronic disease priorities, grant development, training, surveillance quality, evidence translation, and partnerships that improve population health.

<div class="modern-callout">
  <h3>Founding Direction</h3>
  <p>This initiative is being developed as a future public health organization and collaboration platform. The first phase is to build credible focus areas, partner pathways, project concepts, and public-facing resources before formal organizational launch.</p>
  <a class="button primary" href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">Discuss a Collaboration</a>
  <a class="button secondary" href="{{ '/ai-public-health-portal/' | relative_url }}">Open Impact Hub</a>
</div>

## Mission

To help communities, programs, academic teams, and partners turn evidence into practical public health action through stronger data, clearer communication, useful training, and implementation-ready project support.

## How The Collaborative Can Support Partners

- Public health needs assessments and evidence scans.
- Grant concept notes, proposal support, and project planning.
- Infectious disease, chronic disease, cancer, epidemiology, and data quality improvement support.
- Workforce training, teaching materials, and competency-aligned learning.
- Plain-language briefs on emerging public health issues.
- Evaluation plans, dashboards, reporting templates, and partner updates.
- Academic, community, public health, and industry collaboration scoping.

## Operating Principles

<div class="impact-strip">
  <div class="impact-item">
    <strong>Evidence to Action</strong>
    <span>Work should move from data and literature toward decisions, implementation, and measurable public health value.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="impact-item">
    <strong>Equity and Trust</strong>
    <span>Projects should recognize community context, structural barriers, communication needs, and responsible use of information.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="impact-item">
    <strong>Capacity Building</strong>
    <span>The goal is not only to deliver a product, but to strengthen people, teams, systems, and future public health work.</span>
  </div>
</div>
