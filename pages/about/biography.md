# Biography

{: .lead }
**Rauf Shah, MS, PhD** is a **Lead Health Data Coordinator II** working at the intersection of **clinical genomics, oncology bioinformatics, and public health**. He designs **secure, reproducible pipelines** that link **registries, EHR, digital pathology, and genomics** via **FHIR/HAPI** to support **cancer surveillance**, **ctDNA/lymphoma analytics**, and **equity-focused outcomes**. As an **MPH–Epidemiology student**, his interests include **timeliness and quality in cancer surveillance**, **situational awareness for disaster epidemiology**, and the **translation of data into decision-ready dashboards** for clinicians and policy partners. He is also committed to **public-health teaching and training**, including the **design of practice-oriented courses and modules** in surveillance systems, FHIR data engineering, and reproducible analytics.

---

## Current roles & interests
- **Lead Health Data Coordinator II (Oncology)** — registry/EHR/genomics integration, outcomes dashboards, governance & QA  
- **MPH–Epidemiology** — methods, surveillance systems, policy translation, and evaluation  
- **ctDNA & lymphoma analytics** — copy-number–anchored panels, cohort curation, reproducible reporting  
- **Public-health teaching & training** — course/module design (surveillance, FHIR/HAPI, reproducible analytics)
