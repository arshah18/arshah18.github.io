# About

I’m **Rauf Shah, Ph.D.** - a **Lead Health Data Coordinator II** guiding the Nebraska Lymphoma Study Group (NLSG) at the  
**University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC), Department of Internal Medicine, Division of Hematology/Oncology**.  
I work at the junction of **healthcare operations, research coordination, and practical quality systems** that help programs show what “good” looks like-and improve without burnout.

I’m a **computational & experimental biologist** by training and an **epidemiologist-in-training** (**MPH–Epidemiology, expected 2026**). In October 2025, I was elected to the **College of Public Health Curriculum Committee**, where I bring a learner and program lens to policy, evaluation, and continuous improvement.

---

## What I do
- **Program quality & accreditation**  
  Plan cycles, map evidence, clarify standards in plain language, and deliver concise self-studies and responses.
- **Data for decisions**  
  Build right-sized dashboards and baselines that support fair reviews and measurable follow-through.
- **Coordination & stakeholder engagement**  
  Facilitate clear ownership, timelines, and feedback loops across faculty, staff, and learners.
- **Responsible use of AI**  
  Draft→review workflows that save time while keeping human judgment central.

---

## Working principles
- **Clarity first** - simple words, shared definitions, usable templates  
- **Fairness** - consistent criteria, specific and actionable feedback  
- **Right-sized effort** - collect evidence once, reuse it well  
- **Trust & collaboration** - co-design with the people doing the work  
- **Measure what matters** - bring research rigor to educational and operational improvement
