# CPH 705: Public Health Teaching and Training

## Course Overview
**CPH 705 PUBLIC HEALTH TEACHING AND TRAINING**

This course is designed to develop graduate students' teaching skills and strategies necessary to meet the professional demands of conducting public health teaching and training. Major topics covered include adult learning theory, instructional design, needs analysis, training program evaluation, and creating teaching modules for in-person, synchronous, and asynchronous environments.

## Key Projects & Assignments

### Comprehensive Needs Assessment - Cancer Surveillance Training Program
**Instructional Design Challenge**: Develop a training program for public health professionals working with cancer surveillance data, applying adult learning principles and systematic needs assessment approaches.

**Target Audience Analysis**
- Graduate public health students with mixed methodological backgrounds
- Oncology fellows transitioning to population health perspectives
- Research professionals expanding into cancer surveillance analytics
- Public health practitioners requiring updated surveillance skills

**Needs Assessment Methodology**

**Existing Data Sources**
- Analysis of public health curriculum gaps and competencies
- Review of existing course evaluations and student feedback
- Assessment of professional development needs in cancer surveillance
- Evaluation of related training program syllabi and outcomes

**Primary Data Collection**
- Pre- and post-training competency assessments
- Structured interviews with public health educators and practitioners
- Focus groups with potential learners from diverse professional backgrounds
- Direct observation of current training delivery challenges
- Skill gap analysis across different learner subgroups

**Instructional Design Framework**
- Application of adult learning theories (Andragogy, Transformative Learning)
- Development of case-based learning scenarios using real surveillance data
- Creation of progressive skill-building exercises
- Design of multiple assessment modalities
- Integration of Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles

**Evaluation Strategy**
- Kirkpatrick's Four-Level Training Evaluation Model implementation
- Mixed-methods analysis of learning outcomes
- Longitudinal tracking of skill application in professional practice
- Stakeholder satisfaction and program impact assessment

### Backward Design Project - Public Health Teaching Portfolio
**Learning Experience Design**: Create a comprehensive teaching portfolio for a public health topic, applying backward design principles from learning outcomes through assessment to instructional activities.

**Stage 1: Learning Outcomes Development**
- Define measurable learning objectives aligned with public health competencies
- Establish clear criteria for successful knowledge and skill demonstration
- Identify authentic assessment tasks relevant to public health practice
- Map learning outcomes to professional standards and competencies

**Stage 2: Assessment Evidence Design**
- Development of multiple assessment types (formative and summative)
- Creation of authentic evaluation tools mirroring public health practice
- Design of competency-based skill demonstrations
- Implementation of peer and self-assessment mechanisms

**Stage 3: Learning Activities Planning**
- Sequencing of instructional activities to build toward learning outcomes
- Integration of active learning strategies and collaborative exercises
- Application of technology-enhanced learning tools and platforms
- Development of inclusive participation and engagement methods

### Teaching Module Development - Multi-Platform Delivery
**Multi-Modal Teaching Portfolio**: Create adaptable teaching modules for delivery across in-person, synchronous online, and asynchronous formats.

**Module Components**
- **In-Person Session Designs**: Interactive workshops, case discussions, hands-on exercises
- **Synchronous Online Formats**: Virtual classroom activities, real-time collaboration, live demonstrations
- **Asynchronous Materials**: Self-paced learning modules, discussion forums, recorded content
- **Assessment Adaptations**: Flexible evaluation methods for different delivery modes
