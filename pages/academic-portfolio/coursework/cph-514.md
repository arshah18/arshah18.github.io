# CPH 514 - Planning & Evaluation

{: .lead }
A practical course on designing, planning, and evaluating public health programs in real-world settings, grounded in systems thinking, evidence use, implementation planning, and sustainability.

---

## Course Overview
**What this course builds:** a toolkit to move from a clearly defined public health problem and community need to a feasible intervention with a defensible plan for implementation, evaluation, and sustainability.

**How it is approached:** understand problems as part of complex systems, build logic models that connect activities to outcomes, use qualitative and quantitative evidence to prioritize, and design right-sized evaluations.

---

## What This Supports
- Public health program leadership and evaluation.
- Needs assessment and evidence-informed planning.
- Logic models, indicators, implementation quality, and sustainability.
- Communication for program leaders, partners, and stakeholders.

---

## Where This Applies
- **Local/state health departments:** program planning, quality improvement, reporting.
- **Hospitals & health systems:** CHNA-aligned strategies; implementation evaluation.
- **Community-based organizations:** grant-funded program design, budgeting, outcomes tracking.
- **Policy & advocacy:** multi-level strategies and evaluation of policy/system change.
- **Research/practice partnerships:** stronger implementation plans and translation to action.

---

## Signature Assignments & Deliverables
- **Needs Assessment Brief (2–3 pp.)** - problem statement, affected population, priority drivers, feasible leverage points.
- **Program Logic Model (1 page)** - inputs, activities, outputs, short/medium/long-term outcomes; key assumptions/risks.
- **Evaluation Plan (table or brief)** - questions, indicators, data sources, timing, analysis; ties to reach/implementation/effectiveness/maintenance.
- **Budget & Staffing Worksheet** - roles, effort, materials, timelines mapped to activities.
- **Stakeholder One-Pager** - clear, audience-appropriate program summary and how results will be used.

This course supports my preparation for public health leadership roles that require planning, implementation, evaluation, communication, and sustainable program improvement.

---

## Tools & Frameworks
- **Logic models**, **RE-AIM** (reach, effectiveness, adoption, implementation, maintenance), **PDSA** cycles  
- **Qualitative methods** (interviews/focus groups), basic **costing/budgeting**, **equity-aware** indicator selection  
- **Communication templates** for proposals, briefs, and stakeholder updates

---

## At a Glance
- **Course type:** Planning, implementation, and evaluation  
- **Setting:** Real-world programs with measurable outcomes  
- **Outputs:** Logic model, evaluation plan, budget, stakeholder brief
