# Teaching Philosophy

## My Teaching Journey

In my work with undergraduate and graduate students, I've found that **complex concepts become accessible when grounded in real-world context and practical applications**. Whether guiding students through genomic data analysis or public health surveillance methods, I focus on making technical content relevant and approachable.

## Formal Teaching Education

### CPH 705 - Public Health Teaching and Training
My graduate training in evidence-based teaching methodologies through **CPH 705** provided a systematic framework for developing effective public health education. This course emphasized:

- **Adult Learning Theory**: Applying andragogy principles to public health education
- **Instructional Design**: Systematic approaches from needs assessment to evaluation
- **Multi-Modal Delivery**: Creating accessible learning experiences across different formats
- **Program Evaluation**: Measuring training effectiveness and impact

## Core Teaching Principles

### Evidence-Based Instructional Design
- Apply systematic approaches from needs assessment through program evaluation
- Use backward design to align learning outcomes, assessments, and activities
- Incorporate Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles for accessibility
- Implement evaluation methods to measure training impact

### Real-World Relevance
- Use authentic public health datasets and clinical scenarios
- Connect abstract concepts to tangible health outcomes
- Demonstrate immediate applicability of skills and knowledge
- Bridge theory and practice through case-based learning

### Visual & Interactive Learning
- Employ clear data visualizations to reveal patterns and insights
- Create interactive exercises that build confidence through practice
- Use multiple modalities to accommodate different learning styles
- Develop progressive skill-building sequences

### Inclusive Learning Environments
- Foster spaces where students feel safe to experiment and ask questions
- Encourage iterative learning with constructive feedback
- Value diverse perspectives and approaches to problem-solving
- Adapt teaching methods for learners with varied backgrounds

## Teaching Methodology

### Systematic Instructional Design
- **Needs Assessment**: Identify learner needs and competency gaps through mixed methods
- **Backward Design**: Start with clear learning outcomes and align assessments and activities
- **Multi-Modal Delivery**: Create adaptable teaching materials for different learning contexts
- **Continuous Evaluation**: Use data-driven approaches to improve teaching effectiveness

### Case-Based & Problem-Based Learning
Develop case studies that mirror real public health challenges, allowing students to apply theoretical knowledge to practical scenarios in data analysis, genomics, and epidemiology.

### Hands-On Technical Training
Provide guided practice with the actual tools and technologies used in public health informatics and bioinformatics, ensuring immediate workplace applicability.

### Collaborative & Team-Based Learning
Facilitate team-based projects that simulate real-world public health collaborations and interdisciplinary problem-solving.
