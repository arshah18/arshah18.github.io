# Responsible AI Use

{: .lead }
This portal is designed to help visitors think clearly about roles, collaborations, teaching, research, and public health practice without asking for private or sensitive information.

## What Visitors Should Know

- The portal organizes information; it does not make decisions about people, services, eligibility, employment, or health care.
- Any draft text should be reviewed by a person before it is used.
- Visitors should not enter protected health information, identifiable patient details, confidential organizational data, or unpublished sensitive material.
- The best use is a short, de-identified summary of a role, project, class, or collaboration idea.

## How The Portal Helps

- Clarifies the best pathway for public health leadership, faculty roles, research collaboration, industry partnership, or student learning.
- Points visitors toward the most relevant evidence on the site.
- Creates a first-draft collaboration brief that can be copied into email.
- Encourages safer, clearer, more focused communication.

## Future AI Direction

Future AI-assisted features should remain practical, transparent, and human-reviewed. The goal is not to replace professional judgment. The goal is to help people prepare better questions, stronger briefs, clearer teaching plans, and more useful collaboration scopes.

## Responsible Use Principles

- **Clarity:** explain what the tool can and cannot do.
- **Privacy:** avoid collecting sensitive or identifiable information.
- **Human review:** treat generated text as a draft, not a final decision.
- **Equity:** use plain language and avoid hidden assumptions.
- **Professional accountability:** keep final communication grounded in evidence, context, and human judgment.

## Best Way To Share An Opportunity

When reaching out, include:

- the type of opportunity;
- the audience or organization;
- the public health, teaching, research, or data question;
- the timeline;
- what a useful first conversation should accomplish.
