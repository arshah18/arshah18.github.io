# Research Interests

{: .lead }
Research questions that connect clinical context, surveillance systems, epidemiology, and public health impact.

## Epidemiology and Surveillance Quality

- Timeliness, completeness, validity, and usability of surveillance data.
- Methods for comparing, linking, and validating data across clinical, public health, and research sources.
- Clear communication of assumptions, limitations, and practical meaning for decision-makers.

## Infectious Disease and Preparedness

- Public health questions related to outbreaks, emerging infections, preparedness, and response.
- Data quality and communication practices that support timely situational awareness.
- Evaluation of training, communication, and response-support materials.

## Chronic Disease, Cancer, and Population Health

- Trends in disease burden, diagnosis, access, outcomes, and follow-through.
- Stratified reporting that helps stakeholders see patterns without overclaiming certainty.
- Geospatial and survival trends where data quality and interpretation are central.

## Clinical and Translational Research

- Collaborative research that connects clinical evidence, biology, outcomes, and public health relevance.
- Communication of specialized research findings for clinical, research, and public health audiences.
- Publication and presentation work that supports a broader public health leadership profile.

## Methods and Implementation

- Reproducible workflows for public health and translational analytics.
- Dashboard and reporting systems that make evidence usable for decisions.
- Training and documentation that help teams sustain analytic quality over time.

## Near-Term Research Direction

I am especially interested in collaborations that combine infectious disease, chronic disease, cancer, surveillance quality, epidemiologic methods, equity-aware reporting, and practical translation for public health or academic audiences.
