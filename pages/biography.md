# Biography

I am a health data professional working at the intersection of **clinical-genomics**, **oncology**, **bioinformatics**, and **public health**.  
My work focuses on **cancer surveillance**, **ctDNA/lymphoma analytics**, and **translation of data into clinical and policy decisions** and **public health teaching & tranining**. I lead the design of secure, reproducible pipelines that connect registries, EHR, digital pathology, and genomics through FHIR-based data exchange and transparent analytic workflows.

**Current Roles & Interests**
- Lead Health Data Coordinator II (Oncology)
- ctDNA and registry integration; outcomes dashboards; FHIR/HAPI ingestion
- MPH–Epidemiology student with an emphasis on surveillance, methods, and policy translation

**Selected Methods & Tools**
Python · JavaScript · Node.js · R · SQL · Docker · HAPI FHIR · Keycloak · registry/ICD coding · reproducible research
