# Biography

{: .lead }
**Rauf Shah, MS, PhD** is a **Lead Health Data Coordinator II** working at the intersection of **clinical genomics, oncology bioinformatics, and public health**. His work focuses on **cancer surveillance**, **ctDNA/lymphoma analytics**, and the **translation of data into clinical and policy decisions**, alongside graduate-level **teaching and training**.

He designs **secure, reproducible pipelines** that connect **registries, EHR, digital pathology, and genomics** using **FHIR/HAPI** and transparent analytic workflows. Current interests include surveillance timeliness and quality, equity-focused outcomes dashboards, and incident-driven analytics for disaster epidemiology.

---

## Current roles & interests
- **Lead Health Data Coordinator II (Oncology)** — registry/EHR/genomics integration, outcomes dashboards, governance & QA  
- **MPH–Epidemiology** student — methods, surveillance systems, and translation to practice  
- **ctDNA & Lymphoma analytics** — copy-number anchored panels, cohort curation, reproducible reporting

---

## Areas of focus
- **Cancer Surveillance**: NAACCR/ICD/ICD-O coding fidelity; pipelines that link registries ↔ EHR ↔ pathology ↔ genomics  
- **Disaster Epidemiology**: syndromic feeds, situational awareness dashboards, clear thresholds and communication  
- **Translation to Practice**: briefs and visual summaries tailored to clinicians, programs, and policy partners

## Selected contributions
