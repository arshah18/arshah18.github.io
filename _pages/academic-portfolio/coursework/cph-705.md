# CPH 705 — Public Health Teaching and Training

**Overview:** Develop teaching skills for public health education: adult learning, instructional design, needs analysis, evaluation, and multi-modal delivery.

## Competencies
- Apply adult learning theory; design inclusive learning experiences  
- Conduct needs assessments; design mixed-methods evaluations  
- Build modules for in-person, synchronous, and asynchronous formats

## Key Projects

### Needs Assessment — Cancer Surveillance Training
**Audience:** grad public health students; oncology fellows; research staff; practitioners  
**Methods:** curriculum gap review, interviews/focus groups, pre/post assessments  
**Design:** case-based scenarios, progressive skill-building, UDL integration  
**Evaluation:** Kirkpatrick Levels 1–4; mixed methods; longitudinal follow-up

### Backward Design Portfolio
- Outcomes → assessment evidence → activities  
- Authentic assessments; competency-based demonstrations; peer/self-assessment

### Multi-Modal Module Development
- **In-Person:** interactive workshops, hands-on exercises  
- **Synchronous:** live demos, real-time collaboration  
- **Asynchronous:** self-paced content, forums, recorded materials

## Reflections
Systematic, evidence-based approaches improved alignment of objectives, assessments, and activities, and prepared me to develop practice-oriented training (surveillance methods, FHIR/HAPI, reproducible analytics).
