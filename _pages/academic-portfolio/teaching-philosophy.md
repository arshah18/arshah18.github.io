# Teaching Philosophy

I make complex concepts accessible by grounding them in real-world public health and clinical contexts. My approach centers on:

## Evidence-Based Instructional Design
- Needs assessment → outcomes → activities → evaluation (backward design)
- UDL to improve accessibility
- Mixed-methods evaluation for impact

## Real-World Relevance
- Authentic datasets (surveillance, oncology) and scenarios
- Immediate skill applicability and case-based learning

## Visual & Interactive Learning
- Clear visualizations; interactive exercises; multi-modal content

## Inclusive Environments
- Psychological safety; iterative feedback; valuing diverse perspectives
- Adaptation for varied learner backgrounds

## Methodology
- Systematic design; problem-based learning; hands-on tools (informatics, bioinformatics)
- Team-based projects mirroring field collaborations

## Applied Experience
- Mentoring undergrad/grad learners; technical workshops; curriculum co-design

## Prospective Courses
- **Cancer Surveillance & Informatics**  
- **Clinical Data Engineering with FHIR/HAPI**  
- **Reproducible Genomics & ctDNA Analytics**  
- **Disaster Epidemiology & Health Informatics**
