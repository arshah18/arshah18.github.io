# Leadership Approach

Effective leadership in academic medicine/public health balances technical depth with EQ.

## Principles
- **Multi-Stakeholder Coordination** — bridge clinical, technical, admin teams
- **Quality & Standards** — data standards, versioning, reproducibility, governance
- **Strategic Translation** — align technical work with mission and needs
- **Team Development** — mentorship, clear growth paths, inclusive culture

## Practice
- Lead surveillance system builds; FHIR integration; QA/validation
- Advise interoperability strategies and roadmaps
- Mentor emerging leaders in informatics and data science
