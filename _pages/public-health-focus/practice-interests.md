# Public Health Practice Interests

## Cancer Surveillance
- End-to-end pipelines linking **registries ↔ EHR ↔ pathology ↔ genomics**
- Coding fidelity (ICD, ICD-O, NAACCR) and validated analytics
- Equity monitoring: incidence, survival, and access across subgroups

## Disaster Epidemiology
- Situational awareness via **syndromic feeds** and rapid dashboards
- Thresholds/alerts, field reporting, and decision support
- Clear translation of complex signals into actions

## Data Systems & Governance
- FHIR/HAPI ingestion; auditability; role-based access (Keycloak)
- Reproducible research and documentation for multidisciplinary teams
