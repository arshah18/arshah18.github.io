# Translation to Practice

## Data Integration & Pipelines
- **FHIR/HAPI workflows** aligning EHR, registry, and molecular data  
- **Versioned transformations** for reproducibility and auditability  
- **Standardized data models** for multi-site collaboration

## Analytical Infrastructure
- **Reproducible R/Python** pipelines for QC, modeling, reporting  
- **Interactive dashboards** for surveillance and decision support  
- **Validated outputs** with clear documentation

## Stakeholders & Capacity
- **Co-production** with clinicians/registries  
- Transparent assumptions and limitations  
- Iterative feedback; training and mentorship

## Impact
- **Clinical:** faster time-to-insight; tumor board support; trial matching  
- **Public Health:** timely surveillance; equity-focused monitoring; policy briefs  
- **System:** standardization; reproducible infra; scalable solutions

## Key Projects
- **Cancer Surveillance Dashboards** — equity metrics, survival views, training modules  
- **Disaster Epi Tools** — syndromic alerts, situational platforms, comms templates  
- **Genomic Integration** — ctDNA pipelines, variant reporting, linkage methods  
- **Training Programs** — surveillance methods, FHIR/HAPI, reproducible research
